Visualizing Amazonia Deforestation In Recent Presidential Terms

The Amazonia region of northwest Brazil includes approximately 60% of the Amazon rainforest.

Scientists warn that the entire Amazon ecosystem, the largest rainforest on Earth, is now at risk of systemic collapse from the compounding stresses of deforestation, drought, and climate change (BBC).

Explore changes in deforestation rates and land cover in Amazonia across modern presidential terms in Brazil with this interactive map.
